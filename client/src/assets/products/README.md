# Product photos

Drop photos here named after the product SKU. They are bundled at build time and used automatically;
a product without a photo shows a category icon placeholder.

- `<SKU>.webp`, 800×1000 (4:5), under ~120 KB, for example `NOTEBOOK-A5.webp`
- optional `<SKU>@2x.webp`, 1600×2000, used for high-density screens
- SKUs: `SPEAKER-BT-BLK`, `EARBUDS-TWS-BLK`, `HEADPHONES-OVR-BLK`, `PHONE-PRO-BLU`, `WATCH-SMART-BLU`, `CAMERA-MIRRORLESS`

Record the author, licence and source of every photo in `CREDITS.md` next to the photos.
